🔒 How we handle your content

Your audio: Sent directly for transcription and never saved on our servers. Once your text comes back, the audio is gone.

Your transcript & generated content: Only saved in your own browser (for the History feature) — we don't store it on our servers either.

Feedback you submit: Saved so we can read it and improve CastForge. Don't include anything sensitive.
